# Mandarin Garden — 24-second social film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Chinese can feel like play, at every age and every level.
- **Identity:** Mandarin Garden's paper-cut panda and garden palette; warm ivory, deep leaf green, persimmon, marigold. Fraunces-like editorial display paired with DM Sans and Noto Sans.
- **Hero:** The supplied panda-reading app icon, treated as a real brand asset; a graphic garden built from paper layers carries the learning journey.
- **Motion:** Paper-cut parallax, word-to-object match cuts, and elastic card choreography.
- **Arc:** Curious, lively, increasingly focused, then a calm confident brand resolution.
- **Payoff:** A generous bilingual end card where the panda's book becomes the garden itself.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. Runtime: 24 seconds.
