# 中文寶庫 Chinese Treasure House — 24-second promotional film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** One welcoming place to build Chinese through four clearly graded pathways, with a complete practice loop at every level—and a genuinely useful way to try it.
- **Identity:** Continue Mandarin Garden's warm paper-cut world: leaf green, parchment, vermilion and marigold; Fraunces display with DM Sans / Noto Sans TC. The existing panda-reading icon and jade book seal remain authentic, unaltered.
- **Hero:** The jade book seal unfolds into four luminous learning routes; route tiles then become practice tokens and a long reading ribbon.
- **Motion:** Seal-stamp impact and orbit iris; four-route unfolding with traveling tokens; typographic/reading-strip transformation into a clear offer lockup.
- **Arc:** Intrigue → breadth of pathways → what every level contains → reading depth → transparent, actionable offer.
- **Payoff:** An ivory offer card makes free app/PWA download unmistakable while distinguishing it from the 3-day subscription trial and its payment/renewal terms.
- **Format:** 16:9, 24 seconds. The existing 24.03s instrumental remains a usable fit; no VO or SFX.

## Brand assets
- `public/mandarin-garden-icon.png` — supplied Mandarin Garden panda icon, used unaltered.
- `public/audio/bg_music.mp3` — existing 24.03s instrumental; retained.
- Existing Fraunces, DM Sans, Noto Sans TC/SC fonts and paper-grain treatment retained.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–3.50s / 3.50s — “A HOUSE FOR EVERY NEXT STEP”
- **Purpose:** Establish the product and communicate immediately that downloading the app/PWA is free, without implying the subscription is free.
- **Exact content:** 0.00s jade book seal impact; 0.18s `中文寶庫`; 0.48s `Chinese Treasure House`; 1.15s `Four pathways. One place to practise.` / `四條學習路徑，一處循序練習。`; 2.05s small but strong vermilion ticket `FREE APP / PWA DOWNLOAD · 免費下載應用程式／漸進式網頁應用`; hold to 3.10s.
- **Composition:** Deep jade field. Oversized book seal centered-right, ~45% of short edge with two gold elliptical orbits. Copy column left with safe 6% inset. The free-download ticket owns the lower-left zone; no overlapping text.
- **Artistic idea:** A paper-cut seal stamps flat, then its book crease becomes a four-way route junction.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35 seal stamps from 1.45x with a compact spring and contact ripple; 0.35–0.80 Chinese title snaps in; 0.80–1.20 English title masks open; 1.20–1.85 bilingual promise arrives; 1.90–2.30 free download ticket punches in; 2.45–3.50 orbit expands as a compound circular iris while gold junction branches appear.
- **Transition contract:** Seal orbit and branch junction carry into Shot 02; incoming four routes unfold from the same center while the outgoing title shears away.
- **Assets and technique:** Existing Seal, Orbits, icon, SVG paths and paper grain; Fraunces / Noto Sans TC / DM Sans. No new imagery.
- **Sound intent:** Existing instrumental only; no VO/SFX. Retain original beat bed.

Small footer by 5.5s: `TOCFL steps are locally designed, not official. · TOCFL 步驟為本地設計，並非官方級別。`

- **Artistic idea:** A single branching path becomes four independently graded tracks; route markers visibly climb rather than appear as static menu cards.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s iris lands on the route junction; 0.35–0.75s paths branch in four distinct directions; 0.70–1.20s HSK marker lands; 1.15–1.65s PSC marker climbs two stops; 1.60–2.10s ACTFL ten-dot pulse resolves to `10`; 2.05–2.60s TOCFL markers resolve to `8`; 2.60–3.40s all four labels settle, footer disclaimer unfurls beneath TOCFL; 3.40–5.00s four marker tiles lift toward camera and become four practice tokens, maintaining motion through cut.

- **Assets and technique:** Scene typography, SVG path tracings, route markers and geometric plaques. No official insignia or unsupported exam-affiliation claim.
- **Sound intent:** Existing music only; no new effects. Cut alignment not newly measured.

### Shot 05 / 18.00–24.00s / 6.00s — “DOWNLOAD FREE. TRY THREE DAYS.”
- **Purpose:** Deliver a compelling, transparent CTA: app/PWA download is free; the separate subscription trial lasts three days, requires a payment method, and renews monthly automatically after trial unless cancelled.

- **Composition:** Ivory closing stage, unlike the dark article reveal. Left half carries unaltered panda icon and title; right half contains a broad green free-download slab and a clearly separate, smaller trial seal. The bottom 22% is a high-contrast legal strip with two concise bilingual terms lines; URL is large, centered low. Avoid visual conflation of free download and paid subscription.
- **Artistic idea:** The reading ribbon's fold becomes a physical offer card; two distinct seals—download and trial—land separately, visually clarifying the offer distinction.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.30s incoming ribbon folds to card edge as ivory stage opens; 0.25–0.70s icon/title snap into left lockup; 0.60–1.10s free-download slab grows into its fixed zone; 1.40–1.90s separate three-day trial seal stamps to the right; 2.00–2.75s payment-method disclosure then auto-renew/cancellation disclosure reveal sequentially in a fixed lower strip; 3.00–3.55s URL rises and sharpens; 3.55–5.10s full bilingual offer holds with only subtle orbit rotation; 5.10–6.00s orbit contracts around the book seal to match Shot 01's opening geometry for the loop. No text exits before the ending.
- **Transition contract:** Orbit contracts to opener seal geometry and the background shifts from ivory to jade at loop boundary; the seal returns at the same center-right scale as Shot 01. Compound scale contraction plus color-field match cut.
- **Assets and technique:** Existing unaltered panda icon; existing seal/orbits; CSS/SVG offer panels and typography. No app-store badge or claim of store availability.
