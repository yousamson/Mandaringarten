# Chinese Treasure House 中文寶庫 — 24-second film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Mandarin Garden's Chinese Treasure House gives every learner a clear, graded place to practise for HSK and PSC.
- **Identity:** Continues the Mandarin Garden promo: warm ivory paper, deep leaf green, persimmon, marigold; Fraunces display + DM Sans / Noto Sans TC. Treasure House adds a jade seal and gold orbit rings (from the real web page hero).
- **Hero:** A jade "treasure seal" (book in a rounded square) whose orbits open into two paths; graded tiles climb like a staircase.
- **Motion:** Orbit-ring iris transitions, staircase tile cascades (springs), seal stamp-in.
- **Arc:** Quiet invitation → choice of two paths → energetic climb HSK 1–9 → measured climb PSC 3B→1A → calm brand resolve.
- **Payoff:** The panda icon and the seal sit together; the final orbit returns to the opening size for a loop.
- **Format:** 16:9. Runtime 24 s.
