---
name: Membership access and account controls
description: Paid membership activation, expiry restrictions, platform messages and account deletion requirements.
---

Registration includes acknowledging the monthly paid subscription; creating a login identity does not itself activate membership. A confirmed payment is required for member access. Non-renewed accounts must not access normal member pages until payment succeeds again.

**Why:** the user requested「付費會員一旦未續訂閱，即被限制登入，並提供付費訂閱頁面，確認付費後才可登入，註冊同時確認付費訂閱才能成為會員」.

**How to apply:** authenticate the parent but keep the account restricted to renewal, platform messages, deletion and legal information. Billing needs an authenticated identity, and parents must not be trapped behind payment when seeking help or deleting data. Preserve public free material for signed-out visitors. Scheduled cancellation does not remove already-paid access before its end date.

Parents contact the management team through platform messages, with a management-only inbox for replies. Administrator identity is configuration, not a public privacy-contact address.

**Why:** the user requested a platform messaging feature and designated a management login. They did not authorize publishing that login email as a privacy contact.

**How to apply:** keep conversations private to their parent and authorized management. Do not automatically convert the administrator login into public legal contact details.

Parents may permanently delete their account, including when a subscription has expired. Require explicit confirmation of irreversible data deletion, cessation of subscription/access and non-refundable paid fees, while preserving mandatory legal exceptions.

**Why:** the user requested self-service deletion with acknowledgment that all account data is deleted and paid monthly fees are not refunded.

**How to apply:** stop future billing, remove login/learning/messages, and clearly disclose unavoidable processor financial retention and minimal security records. Never claim that deleting the app account erases all third-party financial records.

Members who have accepted the current policy version must not be asked to repeat policy acknowledgements at every sign-in. Keep required acknowledgements in registration and before checkout.

**Why:** the user explicitly said members do not need to reconfirm.

**How to apply:** use the saved account consent for existing members; do not gate sign-in on per-session policy checkboxes. Preserve the separate checkout acknowledgements.
