ACTFL practice uses ten steps grouped as Novice Low/Mid/High, Intermediate Low/Mid/High, Advanced Low/Mid/High, and Superior. TOCFL practice uses four locally designed bands with two steps each; do not describe those bands as official TOCFL grading.

**Why:** The user approved this mapping so the resource library can provide progressive practice without implying that original materials are official exam content or certification.

**How to apply:** Preserve the ACTFL progression and clearly identify TOCFL level names as site-designed whenever displaying or localizing these tracks.
